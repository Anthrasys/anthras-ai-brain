# Privacy Policy for Anthra's AI Brain

**Last updated:** October 22, 2026  
**Developer:** anthrakys  
**Extension:** Anthra's AI Brain  

Anthra's AI Brain ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a client-side browser extension designed for players of RNGidle ([rngidle.fun](https://rngidle.fun/)). We are committed to transparency and user privacy.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

**Anthra's AI Brain does NOT collect, transmit, store externally, or sell any personal or identifying information.**

Specifically:
- **No Personal Data:** We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, location data, or identifiers.
- **No Browsing History:** The extension operates strictly on `https://rngidle.fun/` and cannot inspect or access any other websites or tabs.
- **No Analytics / Telemetry:** There are no trackers, third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.), or advertising networks bundled in this extension.
- **No Remote Servers:** The extension communicates exclusively with the local game instance in your browser. No external API or remote server is ever contacted.

---

### 2. Permissions & Local Storage

The extension requests the following permissions for specific, transparent local functionality:

- **`storage`**: Used solely on your device to persist local UI preferences (such as Net vs. Gross calculation view, filtering by owned items, minimised state, and window position). This data never leaves your browser.
- **`host_permissions` (`https://rngidle.fun/*`)**: Used solely to inject the companion overlay interface and read the active roll numbers and item buttons directly on the game page.

---

### 3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties.

---

### 4. Open Source & Security

All source code runs locally on your browser. Users can inspect the open code at any time to verify its privacy and security integrity.

---

### 5. Contact

If you have questions or feedback regarding this privacy policy, you can open an issue or reach out through the project's repository.
