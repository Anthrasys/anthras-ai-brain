**Short Description** [REQUIRED]

**Detailed Description** [REQUIRED]
Anthra's AI Brain is an intelligent, real-time companion overlay for RNGidle (rngidle.fun).

It evaluates your active rolls on the fly and computes exact statistical profitability, win rates, and net expected profit (EV) across all 10 shop items.

Key Features:
- Real-time roll analysis: instant scoring and card rarity tier display.
- Exact Net Profit (EV): takes into account shop item purchase costs so you know if using an item is mathematically profitable or an expected loss.
- Complete Odds Breakdown: detailed Win Rate %, Loss Rate %, and Net Win Rate % for every item.
- Best Play Recommendation: instantly highlights the optimal item to use or advises you to hold your roll when items are unprofitable.
- Detailed Scenarios: preview Best, Average (EV), and Worst-case outcomes with resulting score tiers.
- Full Non-Real Math Support: native calculations for Reals, Decimals, Complex numbers (such as 1+6i, 7i), Quaternions, and Octonions.
- Sleek Dark HUD: compact, unobtrusive, draggable, and minimizable interface that seamlessly integrates into the game.
- Zero Lag: lightweight, non-blocking evaluation yielding 60 FPS performance without interfering with game animations.

How to use:
1. Open rngidle.fun.
2. Roll or click any number card.
3. Anthra's AI Brain will immediately show profitability metrics, expected gains, and the recommended play.

Privacy:
Anthra's AI Brain runs 100% locally in your browser. It does not track, collect, transmit, or sell any personal data or browsing activity.

Support:

## Privacy & Data Use

### Data Collection
This extension collects **NO user data**.

- Personal identification information: None
- Health information: None
- Financial and payment information: None
- Authentication information: None
- Personal communications: None
- Location: None
- Web history: None
- User activity: None (only roll numbers locally on rngidle.fun)
- Website content: None

### Data Usage
- Data is processed strictly in-memory on the client machine.
- No remote servers, analytics, or third-party tracking services are contacted.
